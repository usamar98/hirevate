# Student jobs ethical link-building playbook

## Pages to promote

- `/research/student-part-time-jobs` — primary citeable methodology and live snapshot.
- `/jobs/student/us` and `/jobs/student/uk` — country resources for international-student guides.
- `/jobs/part-time/us` and `/jobs/part-time/uk` — schedule-focused resource pages.
- `/partners/student-jobs` — partnership and authorized-feed requirements.

## Best prospects

1. University international-student offices and career centers with outdated employment-resource pages.
2. Student unions, international student societies, alumni groups and orientation resource pages.
3. Public libraries, city newcomer programs and nonprofit employment-support organizations.
4. Employers with public Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby boards and recurring student-friendly roles.
5. Career advisers and researchers writing about student employment or job-data freshness.

## Outreach angle

Lead with the specific broken or missing resource on the prospect's page. Offer the relevant country page plus the open methodology. Explain that Hirevate preserves original application sources, shows only evidence stated by employers, and removes jobs after ten unverified days.

Do not buy links, automate bulk email, exchange links at scale, use exact-match anchor quotas, or place links on unrelated directories. Each outreach message should be personal and useful even if the recipient never links.

## Suggested anchor language

- fresh student jobs in the US
- UK student and part-time job resource
- student-job data and freshness methodology
- source-linked part-time jobs

Use varied, natural anchors chosen by the publisher. Brand and URL anchors are healthy defaults.

## Monthly measurement

- Referring domains and editorial links to each resource page.
- Search Console impressions, clicks, indexed pages and queries for student/part-time clusters.
- Partner referral sessions and job-detail visits.
- Broken, redirected or removed backlinks.
- Outreach reply, placement and rejection reasons.

Judge quality by relevant referring domains and qualified visits, not raw backlink volume.
